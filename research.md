  <p>
    My research primarily focuses on the intricate physiology of <strong>bacterial communities</strong> (such as <em>Escherichia coli</em> biofilms) and their dynamic adaptation to environmental stresses, particularly under antibiotic pressure. I aim to decode how spatial organization and metabolic heterogeneity contribute to bacterial survival strategies.
  </p>

    <p style="font-size: 0.95rem; color: #586069; margin-bottom: 0;">

    </p>
